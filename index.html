Intro

This website will be going over the basics of URLs, the history of it, and the future of URLs. This ranges from the basics of URLs to the main creator of URLs, Tim Berners-Lee. We will also be going over key concepts to help user's understand the subject better and we will also be going over different websites that you can visit to help learn more about websites and URLs. Finally, we will be going over me and this project I am working on.


Key Terms

HTML: Hypertext Markup Language that is used as the standard language used to make the structure of websites.

CSS: Cascading Style Sheets that is used to design and style websites.

URL: Uniform Resource Locator that is used to give each website and page it's own unique location on the Internet.

Top Level Domain: The .com or .org of websites and serve as the highest level of domain within a url. This tells the computer where to begin looking for the webpage.
